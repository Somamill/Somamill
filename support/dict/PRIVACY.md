# dict. privacy

dict. performs dictionary lookup, text analysis, handwriting matching and image recognition on the device. Camera frames and selected images are not uploaded by dict. Camera access begins only after using the camera controls, and can be changed in system settings.

Lookup history stores selected headwords, readings, definition language and date locally. It does not save camera images or whole passages. Clear it in Options. No account, analytics SDK, advertising identifier or tracking is implemented.

Dictionary catalogue checks and downloads connect to the public Kotoba Atlas repository on GitHub and its content-delivery servers. Those services receive normal network information needed for the request, such as the IP address; their own privacy policies apply. These requests do not include lookup history or image content.

The Atlas shared lexical core and English definition pack are bundled for offline installation. Downloaded language editions remain usable without a connection. Automatic update checks happen when the app is active, at most daily; updates require a connection and preserve the working edition until the replacement passes verification.

On Mac, app-update availability checks contact Apple’s public App Store lookup service. They include the app identifier and storefront region, not lookup history or images. Installing an app update remains an App Store action, separate from dictionary updates.

This is a development beta. App Store privacy declarations and signing/notarization still require review for a selected distribution build.
